# Examinations

Trained medics can examine the living and the dead. An examination shows what is wrong with a patient, and an autopsy shows how someone died. Both are unlocked with [skills](../Skills/Basics.md) in the [Food & Health](../Skills/Food%20%26%20Health.md) skill tree.

## Examining a patient

1. Crouch next to the patient with empty hands.
2. Choose **Examine patient** and wait until the examination is finished.

The patient can be awake, unconscious or restrained. They are told that they are being examined. Moving away from the patient interrupts the examination, and so does the patient dying. In that case, examine the body instead.

The report shows the patient's condition.

## Examining yourself

Crouch with empty hands and choose **Examine yourself**. You can check your own condition without help, but you'll see less detail than another medic examining you.

The action only appears when you aren't looking at anything else you could interact with.

## Autopsy

1. Crouch next to the body of a dead player with empty hands.
2. Choose **Examine body** and wait until the autopsy is finished.

The report shows the **cause of death**.

## Reading the report

The report appears in a panel at the side of your screen, so you can keep moving while you read it. Close it with **H**. You can change this key under **FROST → Close Medical Report** in the controls settings.

## Waiting between examinations

The same patient or body can only be examined once every **five minutes**, no matter who examines them. If you try earlier, you're told how long to wait. The same applies to examining yourself.
