The Coal Mine produces coal ![IconSmall](../assets/images/Coal.png) while the [generator](generator.md) in its territory is running. To start the mine, climb the ladder to the control room and use **Turn On**.

![](../assets/images/coalmine.png)

Use **Mine Coal** at the pile behind the mine with a pickaxe or icepick. The pile replenishes while the mine is running.
