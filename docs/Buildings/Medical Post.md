The Medical Post offers a place to care for the sick. Its [upgrades](Upgrades.md) unlock a resting bed and a medical workbench.

![](../assets/images/medictent.png)

### Upgrades

| Upgrade | Cost | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common Care | 1 Fabric | Allows an inhabitant to rest in the bed and recover from common illnesses. |
| Pharmacy | 2 Processed Wood, 1 Processed Metal, 2 Fabric, 2 Leather | Adds a workbench for preparing medicine, tinctures, and bandages. |

These upgrades can be installed independently.

### Common Care

With Common Care installed, look at the bed and choose **Rest** to lie down. Your hands must be empty, and only one player can rest at a time. The bed becomes available again when that player stops resting or leaves.

Resting helps cure **cholera, influenza, salmonella, and food poisoning**. Recovery follows the rate at which the illness would normally progress, while faster recovery from medicine or natural immunity is preserved. Treatment lasts only while resting in the bed.

### Pharmacy

The medical workbench becomes available when Pharmacy is installed. Use it to craft medical supplies; the Medical Post does not provide a workbench before this upgrade.
