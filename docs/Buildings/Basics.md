Buildings can be built by the [governing](../Factions/Voting.md) faction. Building menu can be by default opened with `N` key.

![](../assets/images/buildingMenu.jpg)

Hover over a building to see its description, resource costs and a short placement diagnostic. Inside an established territory, construction uses only a [resource depot](Resource%20Depot.md) in that same territory. Founding a new settlement uses loose resources near your character instead. See [Creating a settlement](Settlements.md) for founding, spacing and resource rules.

Once you select building to build, hologram of selected building will appear. You can rotate the hologram with your mouse wheel. Keep in mind that the hologram cannot be blocked or overlapped by any building, player, or immovable object.
