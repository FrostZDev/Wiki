# Faction leaders

Each faction ([Lawkeepers](Lawkeepers.md), [Faithkeepers](Faithkeepers.md) and [Frostlanders](Frostlanders.md)) can have one **leader**. The leader is appointed by the admins, who can also remove or replace them.

## What the leader does

The leader makes the decisions for the faction's [quests](../Quests.md#faction-quests):

- **Starts and stops** faction quests. The faction works on one quest at a time.
- **Chooses** the faction's path when a quest offers a choice. This decision is final.
- **Claims** the rewards of finished faction quests. The rewards go into the leader's inventory.

Every member of the faction helps with the quest the leader has started. Members can read all faction quests, and the quest menu shows who the leader is.

When the faction has no leader, or the leader is offline, finished faction quests wait until the leader can claim them.

## Losing leadership

A leader who leaves the faction, for whatever reason, stops being its leader. The faction keeps its quest progress, and the admins can appoint a new leader.
